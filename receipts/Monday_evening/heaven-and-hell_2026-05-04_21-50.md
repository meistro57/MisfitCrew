## The Big Idea
Heaven and hell aren’t just religious stories—they’re a nearly universal human hunch that after death, your actions in life decide where you end up. Different cultures, separated by oceans and centuries, all came up with the same basic picture: a good place for good people, a bad place for bad people, and sometimes a bridge or judge between them.

## Why This Is Wild
These sources span 4,000 years, from Sumer to Zoroastrian Persia to ancient Israel to medieval Europe to modern hypnotherapy. They come from cultures that never had contact with each other. Yet they all describe the same core: a two-zone afterlife where how you live matters, punishment is often fiery, and heaven is pure and peaceful. That’s a lot of independent inventors for one idea.

## What It Means For You

This idea probably sticks because it answers a deep question: *Does it matter how I live?* Heaven and hell say yes—and they give hope that wrongs get righted. Whether you believe in them literally or not, they remind you that your choices have weight. That’s worth thinking about.
