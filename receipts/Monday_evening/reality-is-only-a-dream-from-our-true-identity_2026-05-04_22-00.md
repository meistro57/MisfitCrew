## The Big Idea
What if the life you're living right now is just a dream — and who you really are is something much bigger, waiting for you to wake up? This idea shows up over and over across history: that our "real" self is outside this physical world, and this whole waking experience is a kind of illusion or dream.

Ancient Hindu texts compare waking life to a dream. They argue that if dreams can feel real when you're in them, waking life is just as unreal. Consciousness can project a fake body in a dream, so the body you have now might also be a projection.

## Why This Is Wild
These sources span 2,800 years, from ancient India to modern channeled texts. They come from completely different cultures and belief systems — Hinduism, Western esoteric, New Age. None of them borrowed from each other. Yet they all say the same thing: what you call "real life" is actually a dream, and your true self is something else entirely.

## The Weird Part (Hardware Glitch)
If reality is a dream, who is the dreamer? And if you wake up from this dream, how do you know that next level isn't also a dream? The idea can lead to an infinite regress — dreams within dreams. Also, if your true identity is beyond this world, then why does this world feel so solid and painful? The idea doesn't fully explain why suffering feels so real if it's "just a dream."

## What It Means For You
You don't have to believe this to benefit from it. Try treating your biggest problems like dream symbols — what are they trying to tell you about yourself? Next time you're stressed, pause and ask: "If this were a dream, what would I do differently?" You might find that the distance between you and your problems is bigger than you think. And maybe, just maybe, you'll start to sense that there's a part of you that's watching all of this from somewhere else.
