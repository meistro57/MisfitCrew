## The Big Idea
Reality isn’t one flat thing. Different traditions from across history say it’s stacked like a cake: maybe 7 layers, maybe 10, maybe more—but always a ladder you can climb with your mind.

## The Receipts

### Source 1: A Kabbalah-like text (undated)
This old Jewish mystical framework says reality has ten distinct levels called *sefirot*. They’re like steps from God down to the physical world. The idea is everything that exists flows through these layers. It’s not just a list—each level has a job, like wisdom or strength, and together they make up everything.

### Source 6: The Upanishads (~800–200 BCE)
These ancient Indian scriptures describe seven planes of consciousness. The first three are wakefulness, dreaming, and deep sleep. The fourth is a kind of super-consciousness. And the seventh? That’s a state where you realize you’re not separate from the universe—it’s called *nirvana*. They say you can train your mind to climb these levels.

### Source 7: Dolores Cannon, *The Three Waves of Volunteers* (1990s)
A modern hypnotherapist says Earth itself is splitting into different “vibrational layers.” Your emotions decide which layer you end up on. Like a radio station—if you’re tuned to fear, you get one reality; if you’re tuned to love, you get another. It’s all the same planet, but different frequencies create different worlds.

### Source 3: Unnamed “PART TWO” text
This one says multiple dimensions can occupy the exact same space. Your perception is what filters which one you see. So a mountain could be solid rock in our dimension, but made of light in another—they don’t interfere because they’re like different channels on the same TV.

## Why This Is Wild
These sources come from ancient India, medieval Judaism, and modern New Age books. They had zero contact. Yet they all agree: reality is layered, and your mind can move between layers. It’s like finding the same weird dream in three strangers’ heads—a coincidence that’s hard to brush off.

## The Weird Part (Hardware Glitch)
Here’s the catch: if reality is layers, what layer is *that* statement itself? And if you can climb levels, who is the “you” doing the climbing? Some traditions say the climber doesn’t actually exist—the whole thing is an illusion. So you’re trying to wake up from a dream, but the dream is real, but also not. It hurts your brain.

## What It Means For You
You don’t need to believe in 7 levels to use this idea. Next time you feel stuck—angry, scared, bored—ask yourself: “Is there another layer I’m not seeing?” Maybe there’s a higher view where this problem looks tiny. Just the act of asking shifts your perspective. That’s the practical trick: reality might be stacked, but you can always choose the next level up.
