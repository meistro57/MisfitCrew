## The Big Idea
What if this whole world is like a video game—not made of matter, but made of mind? Many thinkers, old and new, have said physical reality is a simulation created by consciousness, either yours or a bigger one.

## The Receipts
**Source 1: "Welcome Home" (Chapter 15)**  
This modern spiritual text says physical existence is a conscious projection—basically a simulation. It claims reality is a product of your mind, not something out there that exists on its own.

**Source 2: Jane Roberts — *The Education of Oversoul Seven* (1973)**  
A character thinks he's living a normal life, but learns it's actually a constructed reality controlled by a higher being. His personal struggles—like choosing between art and money—are just part of the game design. He doesn't realize he's also the architect.

**Source 3: Jane Roberts/Seth — *Seth Speaks* (1972)**  
Seth says physical reality is a "camouflage" you create yourself. Your lives are like simultaneous roles played by one big soul to learn. Time isn't linear; your senses filter out the real stuff. You write your own script and choose your own troubles.

**Source 4: Seth — Session 644 (1973)**  
Human existence comes from mixing identity, time, and space. It's not something you have—it's something that emerges when consciousness, time, and space get together.

**Source 5: Jane Roberts — *The Nature of Personal Reality* (1974)**  
Physical life is a training ground. You have to experience bad things to learn that your thoughts create reality. Your mind survives your body, and you tune into this dimension like a radio station based on a pre-life idea.

**Source 6: *The Ra Contact* Volume 2**  
A questioner compares pre-veil existence (before we forgot we're spirits) to a flight simulator. Ra agrees: it's like training where consequences aren't real yet. The veil hides the "real" part, making life feel serious.

**Source 7: Seth — Session 648 (1973)**  
Human existence depends on Earth being unconscious. Our planet is like a sleeping giant, and our lives play out on its dream.

**Source 8: Dolores Cannon — *Conversations with Nostradamus Vol.1* (1989)**  
Using hypnosis, people in a non-physical state said physical life narrows your awareness. There are multiple Earths at different frequencies, and higher ones are brighter and more colorful. Being in a body is like wearing blinders.

## Why This Is Wild
These ideas come from different times (ancient India to 1970s America), cultures, and traditions—Hinduism, Gnosticism, and channeled books with no connection to each other. Yet they all say the same thing: reality isn't solid, it's a constructed experience. That convergence is hard to dismiss as coincidence.

## The Weird Part (Hardware Glitch)
If reality is a simulation, who made it? If your own consciousness created it, why does life feel painful and unfair? If a higher being made it, why does evil exist? Also, if you're "the architect," why can't you just change things? The idea contradicts itself—it says you're in control, but you feel powerless. That's the glitch: the simulation might be designed to hide itself from you.

## What It Means For You
You don't need to believe this or prove it. Just try this: for one day, act as if your thoughts really do shape your world. Notice what happens. Do you catch yourself repeating old stories? Do you feel more curious? The point isn't whether it's "true." It's that treating life like a simulation might make you more creative, more responsible, and less scared of the hard parts. It's a weird power you didn't know you had.
