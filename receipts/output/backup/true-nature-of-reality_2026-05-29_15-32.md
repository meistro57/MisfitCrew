## The Core Claim  
Empirical reality is a derivative manifestation of a unified, non-material substrate. Perceived multiplicity arises from constrained perception or expression, not ontological fundamentality.  

## The Receipts  

**1. Forgotten Books of Eden (1st-3rd c. CE, Judaic apocrypha)**  
Mechanism: *Perceptual limitation*. Positions the physical world as a subjective illusion masking absolute reality. Maps to Advaita’s *māyā* and Kant’s phenomena/noumena by asserting sensory data constructs a false objective consensus.  

**2. Urantia Book (1934, syncretic cosmology)**  
Mechanism: *Transcendent primacy*. Grounds cosmic reality in non-material values (truth/love), rendering matter insufficient evidence. Structurally identical to Plato’s Forms: materiality shadows ideal absolutes.  

**3. Encyclopedia of Celtic Mythology (2004, Iron Age lore)**  
Mechanism: *Cognitive filtration*. Reality is obscured by mental constructs; neutrality accesses essence. Mirrors Buddhist *vijñāna* (consciousness layers) and Husserl’s *epoché* — both require dismantling perceptual conditioning.  

**4. Secret Doctrine Vol 2 (1888, Theosophy)**  
Mechanism: *Triadic emanation*. Ultimate unity expresses through threefold dynamics. Direct isomorphism with Neoplatonism’s One->Nous->Soul and Trimurti’s Brahma-Vishnu-Shiva: unity necessitates multiplicity without fragmentation.  

**5. Dolores Cannon: Three Waves (2011, hypnosis transcripts)**  
Mechanism: *Perceptual diversity as illusion*. All phenomena reduce to singular essence. Replicates Advaita’s *Brahman* and Spinoza’s *Deus sive Natura*: apparent differentiation is epistemic, not ontic.  

**6. Secret Doctrine Vol 1 (1888, Theosophy)**  
Mechanism: *Spiritual derivation*. Matter is secondary effect of non-empirical source. Echoes Plotinus’ emanationism and Hermetic “All is Mind”: physics emerges from meta-physical principles.  

## Why The Convergence Matters  
Independent traditions across epochs (apocryphal, channeled, anthropological, mystical) converge not just on “idealism” but on *specific architecture*: unity → expression → perceptual distortion. This suggests either a deep cognitive invariant (human minds necessarily model reality this way) or contact with non-propositional truth (direct experience of ontological layers). The latter implies these are discovery reports, not speculations.  

## The Hardware Glitch

Either sensory data is fundamentally unreliable (undermining all evidence) or selectively valid (special pleading). Neither resolves.
