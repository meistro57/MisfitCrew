## The Big Idea
Evil isn’t some spooky monster lurking in the basement of reality. It’s more like a glitch in free will — a mistake that happens when people choose wrongly, but it doesn’t exist as a permanent, built-in part of the universe.

## The Receipts
**Source 1: Session 648, 1973 (Seth Material)** — In this channeled session, Seth says evil isn’t a fundamental property of existence. It’s not real at the deepest level — just a temporary shadow. This matches the idea that God or reality is ultimately good, and evil is just the absence of that good.

**Source 2: Session 647, 1973 (Seth Material)** — Here, Seth explains that good and evil only exist because we have free will. Without the ability to choose, there would be no moral categories at all. So evil is a byproduct of choice — not a separate force.

**Source 3: The Magus by Francis Barrett (1801)** — This old book says humans can choose between good and evil spirits. If you choose to overcome evil, it becomes powerless. So evil isn’t unbeatable — it only has power if you give it. You become what you unite with.

**Source 4: Seth Speaks by Jane Roberts (1972)** — This text says rigid ideas of good and evil trap you in cycles (like karma). Believing in evil actually makes it real in your life. The only way out is compassion — which dissolves false ideas of evil. Your thoughts build your reality.

**Source 5: The Nature of Personal Reality (1974)** — This argues that labeling things as “good” or “evil” restricts your view of reality. If you cling to narrow definitions of good, you miss the bigger picture. Evil is just a label we use to judge others.

**Source 6: Conversations with Nostradamus Vol.1 by Dolores Cannon (1989)** — This says childhood trauma can make people vulnerable to being used as tools for destruction. Evil comes from unhealed wounds, not from being born bad. Power-hungry people are just compensating for past pain.

**Source 7: The Ra Contact Volume 2** — This describes how negative entities try to control others by exploiting their free will. But they can only do this if you let them. Positive beings see love everywhere; negative beings are just clever manipulators without real power.

**Source 8: Chapter 11 (unknown)** — This says the spirit’s true nature is flawless and benevolent. So evil isn’t part of your core self — it’s a temporary distortion. You are fundamentally good; evil is like a glitch in the software.

## Why This Is Wild
These sources span from ancient times to the 1970s, cover Western mysticism, channeled texts, and Hermeticism — and they all agree that evil isn’t a permanent force. No one says it’s a monster you have to fight. They all say it’s a choice, a wound, or a misunderstanding. That’s a huge convergence from totally different directions.

## The Weird Part (Hardware Glitch)
Here’s the problem: if evil isn’t real, why does it feel so real? And if believing in evil makes it real (as Source 4 says), then you’re stuck in a loop — you can’t just “stop believing” in something that hurts you. Also, Sources 1 and 2 say evil is a byproduct of free will, but Source 4 says rigid ideas of it trap you — so is free will a trap or a solution? The logic tangles itself.

## What It Means For You
Stop seeing evil as an enemy outside you. Instead, look at your own judgments — where are you labeling things as “bad” out of habit? Focus on healing your own wounds (Source 6) and practicing compassion (Source 4). That doesn’t mean ignore real harm — it means don’t give evil more power by fearing it. You are fundamentally good, and evil is just a temporary glitch you can power through.
