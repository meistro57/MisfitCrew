Yet they all land on the same weird conclusion: consciousness is the real deal, matter is the side effect. That's either the biggest coincidence in history, or they're onto something real.

## The Weird Part (Hardware Glitch)

Also, if all consciousness is one big thing, then who's making the claim? You — a separate self — are saying "we're all one." That's a paradox you can't escape. The idea itself trips over itself.

## What It Means For You
If consciousness is the main thing, then you're not a brain that happens to think — you're awareness that happens to have a brain. That changes what matters. Your real problems aren't about getting stuff or fixing your body. They're about paying attention to what you're paying attention to. Meditation, reflection, questioning your own thoughts — those aren't hobbies. They're basic maintenance. And if telepathy is real? Then try genuinely wondering what someone else is thinking before they speak. You might be surprised.
