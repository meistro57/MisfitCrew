Also, if it's just a metaphor for psychological change, why does it come with physical sensations (heat, pressure, pain)? And if it's a physical energy, why can't we measure it?

## What It Means For You
Don't treat this as a party trick or something to chase. The consistent message across all these sources is: if you feel something stirring in your spine or energy centers, slow down. Seek wise guidance (a teacher, a therapist, a grounded friend). Focus on cleaning up your life—your thoughts, your habits, your relationships. Whether it's a literal energy or a powerful metaphor, the same advice works: prepare the ground before you try to grow the tree.
