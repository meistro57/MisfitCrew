## The Big Idea
There’s a part of your mind that acts like a CEO — it decides what you pay attention to, coordinates your body and thoughts, and keeps everything running smoothly. Almost every ancient tradition and modern science agrees: we have a “central executive” that runs the show.

## The Receipts

**Source 12 (Chapter 11):** Says the conscious mind is a “coordinating agent” that lines up your abilities with what you believe is real. So your executive network doesn’t just react — it shapes how you see the world.

## Why This Is Wild
These sources come from totally different times and places: an ancient Indian text (Vedanta), a 1970s channeled session, modern neuroscience (via the “central executive” concept), and a general medical text. They had no way of talking to each other, yet they all describe the same thing — a central boss in the mind/body system. That’s like finding the same recipe in cookbooks from three different planets.

## The Weird Part (Hardware Glitch)
Here’s the catch: this executive network isn’t always in charge. Sometimes your body runs on autopilot (like when you breathe while asleep), and sometimes your thoughts race without your permission. And when you try to “decide” to focus, who is doing the deciding? It’s like the CEO is also the company — you can’t step outside yourself.

## What It Means For You
You can train your central executive the same way you train a muscle. When you meditate, practice deep focus, or even just pause before reacting, you’re strengthening the part of you that coordinates everything. Over time, you get better at catching yourself when you’re distracted, and your health and thinking become more balanced. It’s not magic — it’s practice.
