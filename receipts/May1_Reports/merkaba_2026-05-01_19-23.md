## The Big Idea (plain English, 2-3 sentences max)
The Merkaba is a kind of spiritual vehicle – a chariot or light-body that can carry your consciousness to higher realms. People from ancient Jewish mystics to modern channelers all describe the same thing: a spinning, multi-faced energy structure that helps you travel between worlds or connect with the divine.
