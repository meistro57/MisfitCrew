The Merkaba is a divine vehicle or chariot that carries a person’s spirit or the presence of God. In Jewish mysticism it’s the throne Ezekiel saw, but other traditions describe it as a light‑body, a UFO, or an energy machine. The core idea is the same: there’s a spiritual transport system that lets you move between worlds or levels of reality.

That makes the whole idea loop back on itself: you have to be the thing you’re looking for.

## What It Means For You

When you meditate on a geometric shape (a star tetrahedron, a pyramid, a wheel), you’re essentially programming your consciousness to travel. The real takeaway is that every tradition says the same thing: your awareness is a chariot. You just have to climb in and steer.
