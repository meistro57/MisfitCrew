And if the Upanishads are right, a tiny entity inside a geometric structure controls everything — but then who controls that entity? The theory keeps adding layers, and you never reach a bottom. It’s infinite turtles all the way down.

Over time, you might notice your own personal cortex — and maybe even find the hidden parts you didn’t know were there.
