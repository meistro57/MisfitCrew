The idea is that you don’t live just one life. There are many other “yous” living different lives at the exact same time – in different bodies, different places, maybe even different universes. And somehow, you can tap into their experiences like data.

**Source 8: Dolores Cannon, *Convoluted Universe Book Five* (2005)**

## What It Means For You

But the idea can be useful: if there are other versions of you who made different choices, you can imagine what they learned and apply it to your own life. Next time you’re stuck, ask yourself, “What would my parallel self do – the one who took the risk, or the one who stayed safe?” That mental jump might give you a new path. You don’t need metaphysics; you just need to act as if those other selves exist and borrow their wisdom.
